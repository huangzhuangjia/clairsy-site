Clairsy · HRV stress monitor for Apple Watch

Your stress, like the weather.

Qingqing stays with you, learning you — your most understanding health companion.

Clairsy reads heart rate variability and heart rate from your Apple Watch, compares them with your own baseline, and tells you how you're doing right now — Chill, Steady, Tense or Stressed. Qingqing changes with you. Everything is calculated on your iPhone and Apple Watch.

Free: see your stress level and Daily Vigor %.

Requires iPhone with iOS 18 or later and a paired Apple Watch (watchOS 11 or later).

Screenshots from the current app (demo data).

01

Stress, plain and clear

HRV and resting heart rate become four clear levels — Chill, Steady, Tense, Stressed — with a stress index and 7-day average. Quick mood check-ins calibrate it to how you actually feel.

02

5-minute HRV refresh

On Apple Watch Ultra 4 and Series 12, with Recovery HRV authorized, readings refresh about every 5 minutes and Clairsy switches to fast refresh, so “Right now” stays current. Your watch face and complications keep up as the system allows — usually within 15–30 minutes.

03

Custom alert frequency

Three stress-alert modes: Low (default) sends one gentle nudge when you move up to Stressed; Normal alerts after about 10 minutes in Tense or Stressed; Custom (Membership) reports your current status every 15 minutes to 4 hours. 15- and 30-minute intervals need a fast-refresh watch such as Series 12 or Ultra 4; on Series 11, Ultra 3 and earlier, Custom starts at 1 hour — or use Normal.

04

Daily Vigor drains

Each morning Clairsy sets your Daily Vigor from recovery, sleep and training load — then stress drains it through the day, so you can see how much you have left.

05

Sleep at a glance

Last night’s duration, sleep score, 7-night sleep debt and stages — deep, core, REM and awake — all free, with Qingqing suggesting a bedtime. Membership adds in-depth sleep analysis.

06

Trends you can see

Day, week and month views of your HRV against your baseline, a breakdown of time spent Chill, Steady, Tense and Stressed, and a stress heat map that shows when you tend to tense up. Trends are part of Clairsy Membership.

07

Breathe it down

Resonance breathing (4 in, 6 out), cyclic sighing, 4-7-8 and box breathing, with Qingqing breathing along — on iPhone and Apple Watch. Before-and-after readings show what changed.

08

Your watch face keeps up

Watch face and complications show your current level, with a one-tap “Measure now”, Home Screen widgets, and optional local stress alerts. On Ultra 4 and Series 12, fast refresh keeps it more current.

Meet Qingqing

Qingqing is the little companion on your wrist. When your stress level changes, so does its expression. When you take a breather, it breathes with you. After check-ins, it learns you a little better. Not a coach — a companion.

SUPPORT

Frequently asked questions

Why do I need an Apple Watch?

Clairsy’s readings come from heart rate variability (HRV) and heart rate that Apple Watch measures and saves to Apple Health. iPhone alone doesn’t record HRV, so stress and HRV readings require a paired Apple Watch (watchOS 11 or later) that you wear. Wearing it overnight helps Clairsy learn your baseline.

What is HRV?

Heart rate variability is the small variation in time between heartbeats. Clairsy compares your HRV (SDNN, plus RMSSD on OS versions that support it) and resting heart rate with your own personal baseline — not a population average — to estimate your current stress level. It’s a wellness estimate, not a medical measurement.

My data isn’t updating. What should I do?

If you see “not enough data”, wear your Apple Watch for a few days, including overnight, and check that Clairsy has read access in Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › Clairsy. If the watch or complications don’t update, open Clairsy on your iPhone once, make sure your watch is connected, and add a Clairsy complication to your watch face. The system limits background refreshes, so readings may lag by a few minutes.

Is my data uploaded?

No. All calculations happen on your iPhone and Apple Watch. Clairsy has no servers and no accounts. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I restore purchases?

In Clairsy, go to Settings › Membership › Restore Purchases, or tap Restore Purchases at the top right of the membership screen. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, go to Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › Clairsy, or in Clairsy go to Settings › Membership › Manage Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid renewal. See Apple’s guide: Cancel a subscription from Apple.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles refunds. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

Delete Clairsy from your iPhone and Apple Watch to erase all of its local data. Clairsy never writes to Health.
